The problem

Most small carriers know what a load pays.
Not what it keeps.

Rate confirmations don't account for deadhead. Fuel receipts live in a shoebox. Maintenance costs sneak up. And by the time you add it all up, the "good rate" already ran.

FreightGain connects dispatch, fuel, trips, brokers, and maintenance into one profitability workspace — so the numbers are always in front of you, not behind you.

Before FreightGain

  • Rate confirmations

  • Deadhead estimates in your head

With FreightGain

Built around the freight workflow

Simple tools for tracking revenue, costs, and operational details.

Every feature in FreightGain connects back to one question: what did this load actually keep?

01 — LOAD PROFIT

Load Profit Tracking

Track rate, miles, expenses, and estimated net profit by load. See immediately which loads are worth running and which ones cost you money.

02 — FUEL

Fuel Cost Visibility

Connect fuel spend directly to load and trip performance. Know your true cost-per-mile before you agree to the rate.

03 — TRIPS

Trip-Based Profit Summary

Group related loads and miles into a clearer trip-level view. See profit across a full run — not just a single stop.

04 — BROKERS

Broker & Contact Records

Keep broker, customer, and load contact details in one workspace. Know who pays fast and who haggles on every load.

05 — MAINTENANCE

Maintenance Watchlist

Monitor service intervals and upcoming maintenance costs. Never let a surprise repair wipe out a profitable month.

06 — COCKPIT

iPad Cockpit Mode

A focused view designed for quick checks from the road or desk. Pull up your numbers in seconds — no digging required.
